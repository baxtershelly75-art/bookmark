Older voices

Bookmark draws selectively from traceable works by older voices such as Charles Spurgeon, Matthew Henry, John Gill, J. C. Ryle, John Calvin, Alexander Maclaren, F. B. Meyer, George Müller, Andrew Murray, and other trustworthy historical Protestant writers when a documented passage genuinely helps.

Exact quotations are kept short, attributed, and tied to a verifiable work. Chapter-specific sources appear inside that chapter's Commentary section.
